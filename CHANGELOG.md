# Changelog

## 0.1.0.110021

- Adds slabs, steps and corners for Biomes O' Plenty grass and soil.
- Smooths new hillsides and dry cave floors using the level selected in Terrain
  Smoother, while keeping native soil types, textures and grass colours.
- Keeps exposed grass beneath steps and corners, including snowy pieces.
- Lets shovels flatten grass and ordinary soil pieces into matching paths.
  Flattening a grass step or corner also flattens compatible grass underneath.
- Adds recipes to rebuild two matching corners into one step and two matching
  steps into one slab, alongside the existing cutting and full-block recipes.
- Requires the Building Pieces BOP add-on for its supported natural rock pieces.
- Uses published dependency jars for builds and Eclipse runs.
